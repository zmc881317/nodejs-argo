FIELD NOTE / 01
"When a forest becomes quiet again, the animals are often the first to notice."

Conservation is more than counting trees. We watch what happens after restoration: birds returning to their nests, insects returning to the soil, streams becoming healthier, and food chains beginning to reconnect.

Real success is not a beautiful photograph. It is an ecosystem that can continue supporting life on its own.
